Areas of support

Four ways the work usually takes shape.

01

Fractional Product & Program Leadership

I help organizations navigate the critical phase between concept and execution as an interim leader or fractional product lead. Support includes defining product roadmaps, structuring program requirements, aligning cross-functional stakeholders, and managing the launch strategy.

02

Grant Strategy & Narrative Development

I support the proposal process and end-to-end strategy to translate promising clinical or strategic concepts into fundable programs. Support includes assessing the opportunity, developing the foundational concept, structuring partnerships, and crafting a compelling narrative.

03

Thought Leadership & Executive Communications

I partner with executives to synthesize complex clinical, technological, or emerging realities into decision-ready communications. Support ranges from ghostwriting and thought leadership development to ongoing newsletter strategy and high-stakes messaging, ensuring the output is credible and strategically aligned.

04

Facilitation & Capacity Building

I help teams move from abstract goals to structured action. Offerings include facilitating strategic planning sessions, leading stakeholder working groups, and delivering keynotes or workshops that build internal capacity and drive alignment on emerging healthcare and organizational priorities.
