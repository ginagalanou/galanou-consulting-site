Background

My work sits at the intersection of healthcare, strategy, technology, and organizational execution. I bring a clinical foundation as a pharmacist, including direct patient care and clinical education, to work that has expanded across digital health, policy, product, strategic planning, and organizational leadership. The common thread is translating complex priorities into something people can understand, align around, and act on.

At ASHP, the largest pharmacy association in the U.S. with over 65,000 members, I held progressively broader leadership roles across professional development, membership, strategy, digital health, and emerging technology. I ultimately led the organization’s digital health work, building its institutional response to AI, designing the inaugural AI in Pharmacy Summit, contributing to national policy and stakeholder efforts, leading enterprise strategic planning, and helping translate organizational performance into executive and board-level decisions.

That experience reinforced something I continue to see across healthcare: technology alone does not create transformation. Neither does strategy in isolation. Meaningful change depends on understanding the problem, engaging the right stakeholders, defining clear goals, and creating the structures necessary to move from intention to implementation.

Today, through Galanou Consulting, I work with health technology companies, professional associations, and mission-driven healthcare teams on product strategy, emerging issues, organizational planning, grant strategy, stakeholder alignment, and special initiatives. My role is often to connect clinical, technical, operational, policy, and executive perspectives and translate them into a path that is strategically sound and practical.

My experience also extends into writing, education, and thought leadership through peer-reviewed publications, textbook content, national educational programs, webinars, podcasts, and advisory work. Organizational involvement includes the National Academy of Medicine, pharmacy organizations, civic leadership, and i2x–Connecting EU Health Data. Now based in Athens and working across U.S. and European contexts, I bring these perspectives together around a central question: what will it actually take to move a good idea into meaningful action?
