Areas of support

Four ways the work usually takes shape.

01

Strategic Initiatives & Special Projects

For strategic work that is new, cross-functional, or still taking shape. Support may include strategic briefs, initiative plans, stakeholder mapping, workplans, and executive materials.

02

Product & Program Leadership

For products or programs that need clearer direction, priorities, or momentum. Support may include roadmaps, program design, stakeholder and partner requirements, strategic decisions, and launch planning.

03

Funding & Grant Strategy

For promising ideas or funding opportunities that need to become coherent, fundable programs. Support may include opportunity assessment, concept development, partnership strategy, proposal positioning, and narrative development.

04

Convening, Facilitation & Strategic Communication

For work that depends on multiple perspectives, reaching clarity and decisions together. Support may include convenings, working groups, facilitation, stakeholder synthesis, and decision-ready communications.

Areas of experience

Work frequently draws on experience across healthcare and digital health; AI policy, governance, and education; professional associations and membership; workforce and professional education; and organizational change.

Engagement formats

Structured to match the priority.

Focused advisory

For a defined strategic question, decision, grant opportunity, meeting, or deliverable that needs senior thinking and a concrete output.

Defined project or workstream

For multi step work that requires structure, stakeholder coordination, synthesis, decision making, and a clear handoff.

Fractional leadership

For organizations that need ongoing senior product, program, or strategic capacity without creating a full time role.
